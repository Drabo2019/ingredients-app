Ingredients – Step-by-step guide

Version 0.6.0 · October 2026 · for Android 7.0 and newer

Ingredients checks food for you: scan the barcode and the app tells you whether the product suits your profile (no pork or alcohol, vegetarian, vegan, allergies, health) – and why, with sources. Free, no ads, your data stays on your phone.

Part A — Install the app

Which APK? On the download page: “Standard” fits almost every phone since 2017. If the phone says “App not installed” or “not compatible”, take “Older phones (32-bit)”. If unsure: “Universal” (bigger, works everywhere).

Before you start

  • Be on Wi-Fi (the file is about 57 MB, “Universal” 121 MB).
  • Have at least 500 MB free: Settings → Battery and device care → Storage.
  • The APK from the download page.

Method 1 — Directly on the phone (10 minutes)

  1. Turn off Auto Blocker (Samsung): Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → Off (confirm with your PIN).
  2. Turn off Android “Advanced Protection”, if your phone has it: type Advanced Protection in the Settings search bar. If it is on, turn it off (it forbids any installation outside the Play Store). If the search finds nothing, go to the next step.
  3. Download the APK: open the link in Chrome. If Chrome warns “this type of file can harm your device”, tap Download anyway. Wait for the download to finish.
  4. Open the file from “My Files” (not from the notification): My Files → Downloads → the .apk file.
  5. Allow this source: the phone says “your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source”. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
    Manual path if needed: Settings → Apps → ⋮ (top right) → Special access → Install unknown apps → My Files (and also Chrome) → Allowed.
  6. Tap Install.
  7. If Google Play Protect blocks it (“App blocked to protect your device” or “Unsafe app blocked”): tap More details → Install anyway.
    If there is only OK: open Play Store → your profile picture (top right) → Play Protect → ⚙ (gear) and turn off “Scan apps with Play Protect”, then repeat from step 4.
  8. Open Ingredients. Done.
  9. Turn the protections back on: Auto Blocker on, Play Protect on, and remove the “Install unknown apps” permission from My Files and Chrome. The app stays installed and keeps receiving updates.

Method 2 — Get the file another way

Useful if the download fails or the file is damaged. The .apk file can be sent:

  • by Quick Share from another Samsung phone or a Windows PC (the file arrives in My Files → Downloads);
  • by USB cable from a computer: connect the phone, choose File transfer in the USB notification, copy the file into the phone’s Download folder.

Then continue method 1 from step 4.

Method 3 — USB cable + computer (always works)

This method worked when the direct installation was refused. You need a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) and a USB-C cable that transfers data.

On the phone:

  1. Turn on developer options: Settings → About phone → Software information → tap “Build number” 7 times (enter your PIN). The message “Developer mode has been turned on” appears.
  2. Settings → Developer options → turn on USB debugging.
  3. Connect the phone to the computer, unlock the screen. At “Allow USB debugging?”, tick Always allow from this computer → Allow.

On the computer:

  1. Download Google’s official “Android SDK Platform-Tools”: developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools, and unzip the platform-tools folder (e.g. to C:\platform-tools).
  2. Download the .apk file to the computer (e.g. into Downloads).
  3. Open a terminal in the platform-tools folder (Windows: in Explorer, type cmd in the address bar and press Enter) and type:
adb devices

The phone must appear with the word device. If it says unauthorized, look at the phone and tap Allow. Then:

adb install -r "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\ingredients.apk"

(replace with the real file name; on Mac/Linux: ./adb install -r ~/Downloads/ingredients.apk). When Success appears, it is installed.

To finish: Developer options → USB debugging → off.

Safety: only install APKs from a source you know (here: this page). Always turn Auto Blocker and Play Protect back on after the installation.

After the installation: updates

  • Each time it opens, the app downloads the new version in the background. A banner “A new version of the app is ready” offers Restart to install it.
  • At the very bottom of the home screen, the App version block shows the version and a Check for an update button.
  • No new APK needed, unless you are explicitly asked.

Part B — Use the app

1The home screen

  • The language (Français, Deutsch or English) is set in My profile → Language. On first launch, the app uses the phone’s language.
  • Check for: shows whose profile is used (when there are several). Tap a name to switch.
  • The big green Scan a barcode button is the quickest way.
  • Below: Photograph a label, Search for a product, History and favourites, Shopping list and My profile.

2Create the profiles (once)

Tap My profile. Everything stays on your phone only.

  1. Under Profiles, “Me” already exists. For another person (e.g. a child with an allergy): type a first name → Add a profile.
  2. The profile in green is the one you are setting up, and the one used for every verdict.
  3. Rename changes the name; “Delete the profile …” removes it (after a confirmation).

3What I avoid, allergies

  1. What I avoid: switches No pork, no alcohol, Vegetarian, Vegan. Under each one, exactly what is checked.
  2. Allergies and intolerances: tap the 14 EU allergens that concern you (gluten, milk, peanuts, tree nuts…). Selected ones turn green.
Without a profile, the app explains the ingredients but gives no verdict.

4My health and My rules

  1. My health: what your doctor advises you to limit – Sugars (diabetes…), Salt (blood pressure…), Saturated fat (cholesterol…), Risky additives. A product high in it then becomes “Check”.
  2. My rules: your own word to look for, e.g. “Palmöl” (palm oil). Choose Forbidden or Watch → Add the rule. Deleted by mistake? An Undo bar appears at the bottom for a few seconds.

5Scan a product

  1. Home → Scan a barcode.
  2. The first time: Allow the camera. Images are neither saved nor sent.
  3. Hold the barcode inside the frame: the result opens by itself.
  4. When the code is read, the frame turns green and the phone vibrates briefly.
  5. Not reading? Type the digits under the barcode → Check this code.

6Read the result

  1. At the top: photo, name and brand of the product.
  2. Three buttons: ☆ Favourite, + Shopping list, ⇄ Compare.
  3. The verdict in a coloured card: ✓ Suitable, ! Check, ✕ Not suitable or ? Unknown (no ingredient list). Below it, the confidence (high, medium, low).
  4. Read the result aloud: the phone reads the verdict out loud.

7Why? and alternatives

  1. Why? lists every reason for the verdict, with the word as written on the pack and its source. If you have allergies, the declared allergens come right after. At the very bottom, Labels and sources + opens the details.
  2. “Check” means the data does not allow a sure answer (e.g. gelatine without the animal stated): look at the pack or ask the manufacturer.
  3. Alternatives for you: Find alternatives that suit me shows products of the same category that suit you and are better on the problem point. No advertising: no brand pays. If your profile has forbidden items (allergy, pork…), the app only proposes products whose list it has checked; if Open Food Facts is overloaded, try again in a minute.

8The highlighted ingredient list

  • red = you avoid it · orange = to check · purple = health: watch.
  • Underlined = explained ingredient: tap it to learn more.
  • Dotted = ingredient the app does not know yet.

9Health

  • Sugars, salt, saturated fat and fat per 100 g, with low / moderate / high.
  • Sugars in sugar cubes, salt as a % of the WHO daily maximum.
  • Then the risky ingredients with their level: Recognised risk, To limit or Debated risk, and their sources (WHO, EFSA…).
  • A healthy product gets Good choice!

10An ingredient’s page

After tapping an underlined ingredient:

  • What is it? – a simple explanation.
  • Other names – how it appears on other labels (E numbers too).
  • What it means for your profile and its effects on health.
  • Learn more – links to Wikipedia and official sources.

11Compare two products

  1. On a product: ⇄ Compare.
  2. Scan the 2nd product or pick it from your products.
  3. Both side by side: verdict, sugars, salt, fat, risky additives, Nutri-Score. The better one is marked better. A difference under 10 % does not count.

12History and favourites

  • Home → History and favourites: every product you opened, readable even offline.
  • Filters: All / ★ Favourites, by verdict (Recommended, Check, Not recommended) and by category (dairy, drinks, sweets…).
  • Verdicts are recalculated with the chosen profile.

13Shopping list

  1. On a product: + Shopping list.
  2. Home → Shopping list: tick what you bought. Remove bought items can be undone for a few seconds.
  3. Send the list: by WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail.
  4. Shopping for someone else? Choose their profile at the top: a red banner flags items that don’t suit them.

14Photograph a label

When a product is unknown or has no ingredient list:

  1. Photograph a label (or, in the result, Photograph the ingredient list).
  2. Photograph the “Zutaten” paragraph flat, sharp and well lit.
  3. Check the text read and fix wrong letters → Analyse or Use for this product (kept for next time).

15Add a product to Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts is the free database behind the app. A product you add will then be recognised for everyone.

  1. Unknown product: Add this product to Open Food Facts. Product without a list: Complete on Open Food Facts.
  2. Once: create a free account on openfoodfacts.org and enter your user name (not the e-mail) and password in the app. They stay encrypted on the phone.
  3. Take the photos (front, ingredients, nutrition facts), check the text, accept the free licence → Send to Open Food Facts.

16Report an error, updates, delete everything

  • Report an error (at the bottom of every result): your mail app opens with a ready-made e-mail. Your profile is never sent.
  • Updates arrive by themselves: when “A new version of the app is ready” appears, tap Restart. When a new APK must be installed, the home screen shows “New version available” → Download the new version; your settings are kept.
  • My profile → My data → Delete everything: profiles, history, favourites, shopping list and account are erased from the phone.

Part C — Good to know

  • The app replaces neither the pack, nor your doctor, nor a religious authority. It certifies nothing. With a severe allergy, always read the label.
  • When it doesn’t know, it says so (“Unknown”, low confidence).
  • No ads, no required account. Product data: Open Food Facts (ODbL licence). To read a photo, the image is sent to the OCR.space service; your profile, never.
